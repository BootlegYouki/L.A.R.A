**Learner Devices:** The project adopts a bring-your-own-device (BYOD) model where learners use their family-owned Android smartphones for classroom participation and home study. Market data from IDC and Canalys (2024) indicates that entry-level smartphones (Infinix, TECNO, itel, realme) with 3 GB to 4 GB RAM are widely available in Philippine households at price points under ₱5,500. These devices are not procured by the project and therefore are not included in the development cost. The desktop client serves learners using school computer laboratory PCs and teachers using their own laptops, similarly requiring no additional hardware purchase.

## Paperless Cost Savings

The L.A.R.A. system eliminates recurring expenditures for paper-based assessment materials. In traditional classroom practice, Philippine public school teachers personally shoulder the costs of photocopying daily worksheets, weekly formative quizzes, and quarterly summative examinations. A typical class of 40 learners receiving one printed assessment per week across a 40-week school year consumes approximately 1,600 sheets of paper, plus toner and machine access fees.

By implementing digital quiz delivery with instant auto-grading and on-screen homework submission via smartphone camera, the system achieves a 100% reduction in paper and reproduction costs for assessment activities. Teachers retain the saved funds for other instructional materials or personal expenses. This paperless model also reduces the logistical burden of physical test paper security, manual scoring labor, and long-term grade record storage, while enabling immediate digital backup of learner progress to USB flash drives for disaster recovery and administrative reporting purposes.
