# 3.2.2 Proposed Project Development Cost

The proposed budget covers the equipment and supporting activities for one pilot classroom. Learners use their existing household phones, and available school or personal computers provide access to the Desktop app; these access devices are therefore excluded from project procurement. *Table 3.x. Proposed project development budget in Philippine pesos, using October 2026 estimates.*

The hardware quantities represent one pilot installation. The printing allowance is a budgeting assumption and does not establish the final respondent count or sampling method. Labor is an in-kind contribution: a zero cash entry does not imply that development time has no economic value. No software subscription is included. Distribution notices and the license of the selected AI model must be checked separately from the llama.cpp runtime license; optional remote access is outside the pilot evaluation and its possible operating costs are excluded.

The software cash estimates are based on their published licenses. Tauri and Rust provide MIT/Apache-2.0 licensing options; Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and TypeScript use Apache-2.0; React, llama.cpp, Vite and Tailwind CSS use MIT licensing; SQLite is dedicated to the public domain.[^licenses] These licenses do not establish prices for hardware, support services or model weights.

**Paperless cost savings.** The evaluation compares paper and reproduction costs for a simulated quiz and worksheet cycle with the corresponding digital workflow. For the materials replaced by that workflow, the project target is complete elimination of printing costs. The comparison records the paper quantity and reproduction cost of the equivalent printed activity and the remaining cost of the digital activity. It does not assume a measured saving for an entire school year, eliminate survey-printing costs, or claim that the target has already been achieved. The observed comparison belongs in Chapter IV.
