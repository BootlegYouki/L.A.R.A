# 3.1.3 Population and Sample Size

Consistent with the descriptive-developmental research design, using an Agile (Scrum-based) iterative development model in Section 3.1.1, the proposed evaluation involves Teachers and Learners who will use L.A.R.A. in the approved study setting. The development model covers Sprint 0 through Sprint 6, with parallel Hub, Mobile app and Desktop app workstreams and review and integration at sprint boundaries. Respondent participation concerns the descriptive evaluation of the resulting system; development roles and simulated load-test clients do not constitute a human respondent sample.

**Target and accessible populations.** The product is intended for Philippine public schools serving Grades 1–12. The field-evaluation plan in QA Issue #22 specifically concerns DepEd elementary Teachers and Learners in Grades 1–6, including a comparison between Grades 1–3 and Grades 4–6. This planned evaluation population is narrower than the product's intended population. The accessible population comprises the eligible Teachers and Learners available through the approved study locale, but the reviewed sources do not identify the school, its participating grade levels or the eligible population counts.

**Respondent roles and planned sample size.** QA Issue #22 specifies administration of the ten-item SUS to at least 15 DepEd elementary Teachers after live classroom testing. Learners will participate in task-completion and time-on-task evaluation under the approved procedures. Section 3.2.4 proposes one pilot class of about 40 Learners. These figures describe different planned activities and do not establish final samples, the number of usable questionnaire responses or completed recruitment. No verified actual participation count is available in the reviewed sources. The ISO/IEC 25010 questionnaire respondent group remains unresolved in Section 3.1.2; it is not assumed to include every group in Table 3.x.

The proposed single-class pilot does not establish how both elementary grade groups will be represented in the comparison required by QA Issue #22. The 40 concurrent submissions used in technical stress tests are simulated system load and cannot establish the number or grade distribution of human participants.

**Sampling and recruitment.** The reviewed sources specify planning figures but do not approve a sampling method, recruitment procedure or statistical sample-size calculation. Consequently, neither a sampling technique nor a sample-size formula is asserted. A formula can be included only if the approved research plan identifies it and supplies verified population values and calculation parameters. The rationale for the final sample must follow the approved method rather than be inferred from the availability of a pilot class.
