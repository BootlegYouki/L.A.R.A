# 3.2.1.4 Entity-Relationship Diagram

The entity-relationship diagram presents the Hub's 19 master tables, their columns, primary keys, and declared foreign keys. It is derived directly from `contracts/schema/server_master.sql`; the offline client schema and SQLx's migration bookkeeping are outside this figure. Crow's Foot connectors express the cardinalities supported by the declared foreign keys and their nullability.

![Figure 3.x: Entity-relationship diagram of the Hub master database.](diagrams/erd.png)

*Figure 3.x. Entity-relationship diagram of the Hub master database. Editable source: [erd.drawio](diagrams/erd.drawio). PNG export is pending the manual draw.io layout pass.*

The figure marks `PK` for a primary-key column and `FK` for a declared foreign-key column; a column can carry both markers. It lists each column's SQLite type and nullability. A required foreign key associates each child row with exactly one parent; a nullable foreign key permits zero or one parent. A parent may have zero to many children on each individual foreign-key relationship. The schema's compound uniqueness constraints impose additional limits on combinations, such as one attempt per quiz and Learner; these constraints do not make an individual `quiz_id` or `student_id` relationship one-to-one. The composite primary key of `classroom_teachers` is marked on both component columns.

*Table 3.x. Purpose of each entity in the Hub master database.*

Only declared relationships are drawn. In particular, `quizzes.topic_id` is listed as a nullable column without an `FK` marker or connector because the authoritative SQL declares no foreign key for it. The `classroom_id`, `student_id`, and entity identifiers in `sync_revisions` are likewise not declared foreign keys. `hub_meta` is an independent key-value table. Neither apparent naming similarity nor an application-level association introduces an additional relationship into the figure.

PIN hashes remain within the Hub and are excluded from client representations. Learner-facing quiz types exclude `correct_answer` and accepted synonyms; however, the contract explicitly permits the owning Teacher to retrieve `TeacherQuiz`, including keys. That contract exception requires the clarification below rather than an unsupported blanket statement that keys never leave the Hub. Hub storage paths and the Admin account are also excluded from client synchronization. The retention policy deactivates accounts and archives classrooms and assignments instead of destroying graded records. This policy does not mean that every row is archived: non-grade materials, topics, and announcements can be deleted under the documented rules. The SQL's `ON DELETE` actions describe referential behavior and do not authorize deletion of protected graded records.
