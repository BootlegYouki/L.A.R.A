L.A.R.A · Ask and answer flow

Asking the tutor: Option A on the Hub, Option B on the device

A learner asks about the open lesson. Option A sends the question to the Hub, which retrieves, builds the prompt and streams tokens. Option B retrieves from the synced chunks and answers with a small model on the phone or laptop. Both stay open until the Lead decides.
