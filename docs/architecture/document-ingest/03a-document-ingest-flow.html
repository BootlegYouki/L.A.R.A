L.A.R.A · Document ingest flow

Document ingest: from the teacher upload to chunks on every device

The Hub parses a PDF or DOCX once, cleans it, cuts it into heading-aware chunks and syncs them like any other content, so the on-device tutor can retrieve from them. Failure paths are dashed red.

Sources: server/docs/document_chunking.md, backend/src/routes/materials.rs, contracts/schema/server_master.sql, rules/database-and-sync.md §3, docs/PRD.md FR-3.2, PR #337, PR #349. Experiment results for cleanup and chunking are pending.
