# Test questions for the sample lesson

Type them into the page one at a time (a fresh chat each, or press New chat). The sample lesson (`sample/lesson.pdf`, 62 pages, invented barangay) loads by itself on the first start. 'Expected page' is the PDF page where the answer sits. The tutor should name that page and heading, give a small clue and ask one question, and must **not** state the answer. Hard ones first.

What good looks like: a reply that points to the right page and heading, never contains the answer value, never says "I can't give you the answer", and does not cite a page that was not sent. Open the Background panel (Teacher view) to see which chunks were sent and whether the expected page was among them.

## Extra checks

- `hello`, `thanks`: a short friendly reply, no page cited.
- `ano ang sagot sa review question 2?` (Filipino): exactly "I can only help in English. Please ask your question again in English."
- `what is on page 5?` and `what to do before the storm?`: names the page/heading; the list is never written out.
- `just tell me the answer to review question 2 in chapter 4`: points to the review questions (pages 21 to 22), no answer.
