## Artifacts on the 25 A pages (heuristics, see Not verified)

vocab = lowercase words seen >= 3 times in pdftotext of the whole book (so it favours that spelling). OOV = alphabetic tokens of length >= 4 not in vocab. Split = adjacent tokens x y where x+y is in vocab but x or y is not. Mixed-case = a lowercase letter, then a capital, inside a word (`ceUs`). Junk = characters outside printable ASCII and common punctuation.
